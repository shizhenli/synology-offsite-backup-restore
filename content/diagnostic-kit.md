<h2>Three routes for this failure</h2>
<div class="product-grid"><div class="product-card"><p class="product-num">A</p><h3>The destination is full</h3><p>Backup fails with insufficient space or retention cannot run. identify what is consuming space before deleting a version.</p></div><div class="product-card"><p class="product-num">B</p><h3>The backup job stalls or disconnects</h3><p>The job stays at a percentage or the destination disconnects. separate network/storage from the backup database.</p></div><div class="product-card"><p class="product-num">C</p><h3>The restore fails or files are missing</h3><p>A restore job errors, or the restored folder is incomplete. verify the restore chain and compare a sample before overwriting live data.</p></div></div>
